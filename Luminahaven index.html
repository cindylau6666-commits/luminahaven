Calm & Mindful Daily Space

Welcome to LuminaHaven. LuminaHaven is a quiet, distraction-free space designed to help you breathe, unwind, and reset during your day.

How It Works

  • Select a mood or focus state (Calm, Unwind, Rest, or Hopeful) on the home screen.
  • Tap to begin a guided breathing session (4s Inhale · 4s Hold · 6s Exhale).
  • Enjoy core breathing sessions with no ads and no subscriptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does LuminaHaven require an account? No. You can open the app and start a session right away without creating an account.
  • Are there ads or subscriptions? LuminaHaven is designed without ads or subscriptions.
